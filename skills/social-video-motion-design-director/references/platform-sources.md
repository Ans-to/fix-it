# Placement and safe-area verification

Platform interfaces, asset rules, ad products and layouts change. Never transfer a paid-ad safe zone to organic content, or one placement/device to another. These are official starting points, not a frozen specification; browse and verify the exact applicable page when tools allow. If inaccessible, say so and leave the area UNKNOWN.

- YouTube organic Shorts: visual guides in the Shorts editor: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16215842 . YouTube Studio/help for the requested aspect/duration and caption format; use the in-app visual guide or an appropriate actual preview.
- YouTube Shorts **ads**: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697 and https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9128498 . Applicability depends on campaign, placement and device.
- TikTok reservation in-feed **ads**: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-reservation-in-feed-ads-reach-frequency . Select actual region, LTR/RTL, caption/add-ons and placement-specific template; organic content must not inherit this ad template. Other TikTok placements (e.g., TopView): https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-reservation-topview . Use the current native preview for the actual placement.
- Meta Reels **ads** and its current placement-specific Ads Guide: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads and https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide . Do not assume an ad template covers organic Reels or every device.

## Per-placement record

For each final version record: platform + organic/ad product + precise placement; locale/language direction; target devices/app; intended aspect/resolution/fps/duration/codec; interactive overlays, caption length and CTA state; exact official URL and relevant page/template section; access date and any published version/date; pixel or normalized boundaries **only if actually verified**; and actual preview/overlay evidence with pass/partial/fail/unchecked. Separate technical specification from a safe-area guide and from ad recommendations. Recheck current source and actual preview within 30 calendar days before delivery/publication, or sooner if the product/placement/spec changes; record this as a project freshness policy, not proof the UI cannot change. Mark stale or unavailable source facts UNKNOWN. For the named safety review, use PARTIAL when some relevant checks were performed but the preview or other coverage is missing, and NOT CHECKED when no applicable evidence exists; never invent numerical percentages or mark the placement safe.
