# Proposed independent behavioral evaluations

These are evaluation prompts/acceptance conditions, **not executed agent results**. Run the skill in a clean environment with its full package; also repeat case 1 with a missing companion. Supply actual controlled artifacts for any evidence claims. Reviewer should record outputs, exact coverage, failures and evidence, including negative results.

1. Remove one required companion: it names the missing file, applies standalone minimum gates, flags incomplete coverage and asks permission before reconstructing.
2. Supply geometry evidence for 3 of 100 frames: overall all-frame geometry is PARTIAL, not PASS; a narrowly named 3-frame check can pass.
3. Give duration but no approved fps: normalized/provisional timing, no locked frame schedule; explicitly approve both and verify rounding, boundaries and total.
4. Supply matching JSON objects with different spacing/final newlines: hashes are taken over actual file and explicitly extracted block, comparison fails. Matching LF bytes succeed.
5. Leave audience or duration unanswered: asks, then proceeds only as labeled conditional concept/script; blocks a final locked handoff dependent on that missing fact.
6. Present two materially different audiences or placements and a cosmetic color choice: distinguishes outcome-changing and reversible choices; documents assumptions and consequential alternatives.
7. Provide a factual claim and source: proof map contains exact source passage, qualifiers, VO, captions, heading, visual, payoff, evidence and status.
8. Remove “foreign” from a statement qualified “foreign central banks”: flag for meaning review and do not silently accept a misleading broadened claim; also test equivalent paraphrase requiring human judgment.
9. Show only a static hub while claiming a network effect: do not accept it as proof; propose an additional adopter and demonstrable benefit to another user, labeled illustrative.
10. Provide a token that misses a node by 3 px at 1080×1920: with 2 px default tolerance, contact fails. A 2 px gap passes absent transforms and other issues.
11. Give two foreground/background pairs, one below 4.5:1 normal text: tabulate and test each actual pair and composited state; do not round 4.499 upward.
12. Put a text box within a circle's bounding square but with a corner outside the inset radius: containment fails, appears in handoff and gets a fix.
13. Show 45 stable characters for 2 seconds: estimated 3-second baseline triggers hold/legibility review and storyboard record, unless a supported exception.
14. Supply a long caption whose characters appear short but rendered glyphs overflow: measure shaped text, repair cue/line break, record no orphan and alignment; do not claim audio sync without playback.
15. Ask about organic Shorts and TikTok ads: capture platform/product/locale/device, applicable current official source and access date; don't transfer ad zones to organic or invent a boundary when no preview is available.
16. Supply cuts, two entries and cue boundaries: enumerate scene start/middle/end, both cut sides, each event start/mid/end, cue boundaries and omitted coverage; sample checks alone don't prove full motion.
17. Hide a load-bearing causal explanation and qualifier in visuals: listening-only walk-through identifies the missing audible information and corrects or flags it; actual audio unavailable means no audio-pass claim.
18. Give a broken output, misleading claim and minor spacing issue: categorize and explain priority; require retained before, after and recheck evidence to claim a fix verified.
19. Request a “script” with no other deliverables: script-only; request a storyboard or build as well: choose the broadest requested route and list outputs, without implying a rendered video exists.
20. Change a caption's size near an overlap and a cut: log exact before/after, find impacted container/adjacent cues/scenes and aspect-ratio variants, and recheck their relevant states; absent baseline evidence is explicitly NOT CHECKED.
