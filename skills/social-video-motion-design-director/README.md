# Social Video & Motion Design Director

`SKILL.md` is the entry point. Install or copy the **whole skill directory**: the five references and four templates are required for full production/review. `SKILL.md` has an explicit missing-companion fallback for restricted use. `evals/` is optional. The skill directs audience-aware storytelling, visual design, motion and production handoff; it does not itself render a video or guarantee engagement, accessibility or accuracy.

The operating contract covers intake/routing, confirmed vs proposed frame math, claim/qualifier and listening-only checks, geometry, reading holds, captions and per-pair contrast, placement-source freshness, checksums, sampling, status and evidence-backed revisions. The templates provide concrete claim/proof, production, review and revision records. Placement resources are starting points, not universal safe zones.

## Mechanical checks

From the repository root run:

```sh
python3 -B skills/social-video-motion-design-director/evals/test_contracts.py
python3 -B skills/social-video-motion-design-director/evals/test_review_guardrails.py
python3 -B skills/social-video-motion-design-director/evals/test_operating_contract.py
```

These test selected illustrative implementations and package paths, not an independent AI agent's adherence, full video output, accessibility, factual accuracy or creative quality. `evals/director-review-cases.md` and `evals/operating-contract-cases.md` are future behavioral evaluation cases, not assertions that an independent evaluation passed.
